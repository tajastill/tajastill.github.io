# Ludens Privacy Policy

Last updated: \[DATE\]

Ludens (“we”, “us”) is operated by \[YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME\] in \[CITY\], Australia. You can contact us at \[SUPPORT EMAIL\]. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Who can use Ludens

Ludens is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account and its data.

## 2. What we collect

**Account details.** Your name, email address and password (stored only in hashed form, never as plain text). You can optionally add a school and a profile photo.

**Study activity you log.** The subject, duration, mood, notes, what you learned, the time of the session, an optional photo, an optional study spot or location label, and an optional song you attach (title, artist, genre and album artwork).

**Plans and groups.** Planned sessions, groups you create or join, and group goals and progress.

**Live status.** If you start a live session, the subject and your study spot or location label are shown to your followers until you stop it.

**Location.** When you ask for nearby study spots, your device’s location is sent to our server so it can find spots near you. \[CONFIRM: we do not store your precise coordinates; only the study spot or label you choose is saved with a session.\] You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings.

**Photos.** Only the photos you choose to upload. We ask for photo library access only when you add a photo.

**Social activity.** Who you follow, likes, comments, blocks and reports you make.

**Technical data.** Our servers keep standard logs (such as IP address, request time and device type) for security and troubleshooting. \[CONFIRM: we do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or tracking.\]

## 3. How we use your information

- To run Ludens: your profile, feed, stats, plans, groups and leaderboards.
- To keep the community safe: handling reports, blocking, and removing content or accounts that break our Terms.
- To secure the service and fix problems.
- To contact you about your account or respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Your sessions** are visible to you and to people who follow you.
- **Your profile** (name, school and photo) can be found by other users through search.
- **Live status** is visible to your followers while it is on.
- **Groups:** members of a group can see each other’s progress in that group.
- **Leaderboards:** if “Show my study spots on public leaderboards” is on, your name and your total study time at a spot over the last 30 days are visible to other signed-in users. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy. \[CONFIRM the default for new accounts.\]
- **Blocking:** if you block someone, neither of you can see the other’s sessions, comments or live status, and any follow between you is removed.

- **Supabase**: database and file storage (for example profile and session photos).
- **Render**: hosting for our server.
- **Apple**: distribution of the app through the App Store and TestFlight. \[CONFIRM: song search uses Apple’s iTunes Search service.\]

## 6. How long we keep your data, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, sessions, comments, likes, follows, planned sessions, group memberships, groups you created, and your uploaded photos.

Backups may keep copies for up to \[X – CONFIRM\] days before they are overwritten. If you have been reported by another user, we may keep a minimal record of the report (its type, date and your user ID number) to prevent repeat abuse.

## 7. Safety and reporting

You can report sessions, comments and users inside the app, and block users. We review reports and may remove content or accounts. Contact us at \[SUPPORT EMAIL\] for anything urgent.

## 8. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you may also have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection, and you can use the same email address to exercise them. If you are unhappy with how we handle a request, you can complain to us first, then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

## 9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords in hashed form, and restrict access to our systems. No service is perfectly secure, and if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you as the law requires.
